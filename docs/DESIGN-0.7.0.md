# Learning interface 0.7.0

The main navigation starts with Learn, Projects and Classroom. Studio and device tools follow. The optional AI assistant sits at the bottom of the rail. Development labels and duplicate headings are removed from the visible workspace; secondary library information and educator discussion are collapsed. The build console appears when an operation produces a message, instead of occupying space on startup.

Manrope is the locally bundled interface font. Jective and Robotics Studio share Orbitron, size, weight and color: blue in the light theme and light violet in the dark theme. Official sources: [Manrope](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope) and [Orbitron](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/orbitron). Their original OFL license notices are included in assets/licenses and the application's license directory. No font download is needed at runtime.

The top-right sun/moon button changes the saved theme. The adjacent speaker button controls quiet synthesized launch, button, success and retry cues. The sound preference survives a process restart. No external audio files or streaming services are used. Electron WebAudio scheduling, running context and suppression when muted are tested; physical speaker audibility is not asserted by automation.

All 17 authored lessons have a logical SVG diagram with a step control and animated highlighting. Mapping, ADC and PWM lessons additionally have interactive sliders; PWM has a duty waveform. Existing timing, state-machine and distance-calibration diagrams remain available. SVGs describe logical roles and learning models; the power diagram explicitly does not prescribe physical wiring. Reduced-motion preferences suppress transitions.

After theory and activity, each lesson offers one concept question and one numerical exercise in EN/RU/KK. Answers rotate rather than always placing the correct choice first. Checking gives question-specific explanations, a return-to-theory link, a score and a retry action. A successful check records knowledge-check-passed in SQLite and survives validated backup transfer. Incorrect or incomplete answers do not record completion. Zero, decimal commas and whitespace are handled deliberately; arbitrary suffixes, exponents and nonfinite values are rejected. These exercises check the stated concepts and calculations, not arbitrary learner programs or physical hardware.

The learning library switches to a single-column article and compact lesson selection on smaller windows. Controls wrap, text inputs remain within the article, and diagrams scale with available space. On wide windows the lesson index stays visible while reading an article. The desktop minimum window width is 760 pixels; Electron interaction and document-overflow checks cover an 800-pixel viewport.

Evidence screenshots: evidence/design/0.7.0. Domain tests cover every lesson's correct and incorrect answers, numeric parsing, common debounce/PWM/missing-reading mistakes and backup preservation. Electron tests cover layout, theme, quiz feedback, progress persistence, reset on lesson change, diagram interactions, slider endpoints and sound controls.
